About prices: every price here is what the asset actually sold for (what the seller received). "Cheapest sold" is the lowest real sale in the last 30 days. 1 UPX sales are left out (they're creators' giveaways and gifts between accounts, almost never a market price), and so is anything under a quarter of the design's usual price. Designs with no real sale in 30 days show their last sale instead and sort after the rest. Upland keeps asking prices off the chain, so the asking price for a listed serial is only visible inside Upland. "For sale" counts serials whose latest move into Upland's escrow was a listing; "In escrow" also counts items Upland holds for other reasons.
